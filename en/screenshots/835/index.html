Remember, iOS has Focus? Do you use it? Tell me how you use it. I used to set up a separate one for work, but something about it did not suit me and I turned it off. In iOS 18 they made more presets, explanations of "how and why this works," and cute pictures. Okay, the pictures are totally generic, the team apparently does not have much of a budget. Or Apple still does not have an established illustration style. The second, of course, is more likely. I like that in the illustration for the Work profile, a person is working from home, in the kitchen. Is this a hint that Apple offices are closing and everyone will work distributed? Or is it an illustration that there is no life except work. What future awaits us? I am for remote. The coolest projects are made by teams consisting of thousands, and soon tens of thousands, of people. There is no need to seat them in one office; we need to explore and try working in a new way: creating a single creative information field And the illustration for Personal makes the heart feel so warm: a person is watching birds. A birdwatcher, it turns out
