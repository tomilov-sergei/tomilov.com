Setapp is an alternative to the App Store with a subscription model. $10 for the entire app catalog. Yesterday's post and the changes in my work patterns would not have happened without this subscription. I subscribed because of Craft and MindNode. The rest are visible in the screenshot. And it kind of pays for itself. Craft is a native Notion alternative, but without databases. It launches instantly and works fast. I love Notion as an idea, but in my cases I see it only as a wiki. MindNode, if all you need is to build mind maps, is a much faster and more responsive app than Miro. And it looks sexy. Paste relieves my routine a little; it is on the third screenshot. It gives visual access to the clipboard. And lets you use it as a stash. You can copy a lot of things in a row, then paste from the "pocket." The Setapp catalog has apps for designers. Flow, as a lightweight After Effects for interface animation. Flinto, once beloved by me, for prototyping. And Hype, underrated by the world — animations and prototyping in an HTML5-friendly format. Try it (referral link). Maybe it will inspire you to feats too.
