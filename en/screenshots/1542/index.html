And now the canonical image is being reborn. A clean white screen, the same input field. But under the hood, different technologies. And while one group of people treats this input line the same way as the old one and tries to learn a set of spells to get a result, another communicates as if for the first time: "Please help me solve my homework", "I want to find..." and so on. And it answers! And what will the interaction be in the end? Will the LLM adapt to our lazy heads, which sometimes struggle to formulate thoughts, and in the mush of symbols bring what we actually needed? Or will our brain catch the limit of interaction possibilities and adapt? We will have dialogues, yes, more complex than "PVC windows", but with an intangible, unverbalizable feeling of limits, and a language of interaction of its own will form
