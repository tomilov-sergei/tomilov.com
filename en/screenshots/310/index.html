Battle of two yokozuna. Instagram has become enlightened. Now you can choose: either you have a feed of posts with women in swimsuits of endlessly disappearing area for only €12.99 a month. Or you have a feed of women in swimsuits for free and relevant advertising. I do not even know what to choose... Thanks to ads on Instagram I bought the best slippers in the world, a shelf for the TV, and tickets to The Nutcracker. Our blog is about beauty. What did I like here? How unbothered the yokozuna draws a pop-up where the illustration is meant to show system content on the screen. Checkboxes next to switchers - easy. To make it more fun, one row is highlighted with a contrasting brand color. A curious technique emphasizing lineage. And on the next step, after pressing the Get started button, we see the second yokozuna. Which, without much effort, crosses out the efforts of brand consistency: "This is Meta! And seven shades of blue."
