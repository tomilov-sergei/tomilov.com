Nuevo.Tokyo released an app - a b/w film emulator AgBr Carefully crafted in Tokyo, based on long-term research and development using real modern and expired film stocks. These are not masks, they honestly render noise with every film change. I like how fast and smooth it works. I like that you can save (export) a photo with one hand movement. The rather large rectangle-box of the selected film does not get in the way because it only covers the lower left corner I want to draw attention to the behavior when swiping bottom-up to access editing functions. The swipe moves the photo a little to the left side - the interface says with its behavior that it is switching to another mode of operation. Elegant! And love for the user: the next time the app is opened, it remembers which film was selected. The app remembers which export format was selected. And it lets you load shots not only from the photo gallery, but also as files. Even in a one-screen app there is room for thoughtful little details. There is always room for them
