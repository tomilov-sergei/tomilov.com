In my beloved Shiffles, vertical navigation is duplicated by horizontal navigation. I like how it is presented as a little sausage of avatars 🌚🌕🌝 But why like this? In our heads, we build a mental map of app navigation. We scroll the feed top to bottom, so opened collages should also be flipped top to bottom without changing behavior.
