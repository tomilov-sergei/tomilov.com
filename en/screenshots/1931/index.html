I Today I was lucky to participate in a conversation with Otto Scharmer He is concerned with rupture - the fabric of civilization is tearing, our ability to sensuously perceive the future that is arriving is being lost. Sensing the future is the core of Theory U. Connectedness between people is tearing. Signals highlighting loneliness appear more often II At an MBTI workshop, Zhenya Volyanskaya highlighted that the distribution of people with extraverted behavior and people with introverted behavior is 70 and 30%, respectively. Extraverted behavior is approved by society. It takes additional work on yourself to identify the influence of society and sift out your true inclination. I saw this in myself: today's me came out as ENFP in the screening. I found my test from ten years ago, there it was INFP. Dima, thank you for doing it back then. Zhenya says the MBTI approach is valuable because the profile is formed by age five and does not change during life III The modern world emphasizes and already accepts as normal that a woman does not have to enter marriage or need a network of relatives in order to have and raise a child. We live in an era when it is possible to raise a child independently. The economic situation and cultural shift of the last two hundred years contribute to this. Whether to rejoice or grieve - we will find out in five hundred years IV I will carefully put forward a hypothesis: what Otto Scharmer feels as rupture is a shift and leveling of the median. Loneliness is normal. Even my extraverted Mikha periodically withdraws into herself: "I want to be by myself." External solitude, in essence, is not loneliness. We have us. Our internal family IFS according to Schwartz. Our part of being according to Wilber. We may, for the first time, find ourselves in a world of abundance and safety where survival and prosperity no longer require such deep integration with the community V A qualitative transformation has happened in our era. We do not need a village and a tribe to continue being a neuron in a neural network. We have the internet Thanks to Egor for the neuron idea
