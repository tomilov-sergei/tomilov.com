On May 24 a voice sounded. This channel appeared. Today it turned six months old. For about 10-12 years I dreamed that I would broadcast some signal of my own into the noosphere. Apparently one can say it is working. I want to share three aspects that I single out more strongly than the others. 1. Discipline. The spark of creativity ignites from external pressure: "I need to write a post" 2. Writing a post is an adventure. I do not prepare, I write in one shot. Jokes are born in the process of writing. The ending is a surprise even for me 3. The channel has subscribers. This is an incredible feeling. It resonates with you, you are interested, you share your observations opened in response. An exchange of experience and world pictures happens. It is captivating Today: screenshots, channel numbers. 💎 The most widely shared post was about digitized avatars of Zuckerberg and Lex Fridman. Seems it was Yovan's repost. Thank you ☺️ 💬 The most discussed was about workflow in Arc. By the way, I still live like that for now. Flight is normal. Not perfect, but previews of other pages inside open ones help divinely. 🎱 It turns out the channel leveled up to level 28. I do not know how to relate to this. Thanks for not being hardcore. This lived experience is dear to me now
