Category icons The animated icons made a lot of noise. How do you make such smooth animations of 3D objects at 60fps in a mobile app and on the web? This is not Lottie and not Rive. This is a new video format developed inside Airbnb. The remaining needed frames are generated on the fly from keyframes, all this supports transparency (checkmate, atheists!) and is packed into one playable file. They call this format Lava The creator of the format, Ramon Fritsch, first told everyone on social networks about the format and mentioned that it could become open source... then deleted his posts. Once, Lottie came out into the light from the depths of Airbnb. Maybe this time too, after the proceedings, it will come out 🤞
