The perfect note-taking tool does not exist. - Notion, LEGO for pre-software, is heavy for momentary notes. It is a standalone operating system that must always be running. Otherwise it responds very slowly. I do not want to talk about the advantages - you know them all yourselves. Today they have grown to 100 million users - The whole constellation of Obsidian, Anytype, and others, where you can build a dependency graph and link different notes. They got stuck on the first step of the linking idea - Craft - natively written for Mac and iOS - got close. They had calendar integration from the start, convenient for keeping minutes. I recommend it. But inside there is no idea of "linking" units of information In short, the perfect tool for creating notes needs to be created! Layout: April, 2024
