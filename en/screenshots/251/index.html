There are 2 years between these two demos. How does it feel? Yes, this is a fresh Lex Fridman podcast tied to the wave of news from Meta's presentation. A techno demo of a conversation based on the already existing Meta Quest Pro. The power of LLMs and large volumes of data. It is curious that the future will arrive where books described it and films showed it. But in a completely different technological way. This is that very hologram, but Leia Organa does not need to send a droid to another planet. Could it be that the technologies and the very need to project information "outward," into the offline world, will not be necessary? And that we will instead build new ways of interaction in a fully digital world? Yes, quite possible. We just need to build a Dyson sphere so we can stop worrying about resources.
