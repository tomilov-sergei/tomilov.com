I simply saw a post in the Xwitter feed. I love games, I am interested in what Sitnik plays. Thanks to OpenAI, we know Andrey plays Dead by Daylight. In a second I felt all the fear of VP-level people at Google. The internet is leaking out of their hands! Okay, let us be honest, Google can catch up in this type of input-output interaction and show the same magic. If it accelerates decision-making processes to the necessary speeds. It is more interesting to think about what is not in today's screenshots. The ChatGPT request input field is a command line. Working as if with a DOS-level operating system. You do not know the magic command - you will not launch Doom. - What will the window into the world of working with multimodal models be like? - What interaction principles will form the basis? - What will this new Windows be like, packaging technologies into a friendly user interface and blowing up our idea of computers, as happened 40-50 years ago? Maybe we are now as if in the days of a gold rush. All that is needed is to find the right field of questions and start sifting sand in search of answers.
