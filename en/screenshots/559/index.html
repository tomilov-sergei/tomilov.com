Danya Trabun came up with Totem. A concept for a wearable device, and it is understandable! The hardest thing in our societies: quality interaction between separate links. Starting a love affair, building a network of contacts, interacting with loved ones. We are slowly and awkwardly learning to do this well. LLMs, even in their current form, feel like assistants that help formulate thoughts more precisely and turn desires into a form understandable to another person. Totem is meant to boost this. The better people transfer ideas from one head to another, the stronger civilizations develop. I am waiting for more and more products like this to appear This is what should have been at CES this year. Sorry Rabbit R1, Pin, and other smart vacuum cleaners. Your creators are enchanted by technologies, not by the human. The project is real creativity. Danya and the team here are inspired by favorite things: The film Her, where the design of things and interfaces is humanized. And a couple of universes: Kanye's, where Earth is space, and Vadik Marseladov's universe, where ideas live in the form of hyper-realism.
