When I started the channel, among other things I promised myself: notice interesting and beautiful things, find promising ones. But sometimes you want an answer from the Universe: Why is this? What does it mean? What does "Before you go to Google Maps" mean? - Before you go to Google Maps, wash your hands! - Before you go to Google Maps, take three deep breaths. - Before you go to Google Maps, do 10 squats. And yet on long tap, quick preview shows exactly what should be shown! What were they trying to say? Google is a corporation of sad decisions. Let us not be like Google. Let us look at our decisions through users' eyes.
