Kino. A new app from the indie team that has been passionately making photo-shooting apps for 7 years. Now for video too. What do they offer? Realtime color correction, pro camera settings, recording to Files instead of cluttering Photos. An external drive connected to the iPhone is automatically recognized and recording will go there. And most importantly! You can lock white balance and it will no longer jump! $10 for that is practically a gift. The team became famous for its craft approach to bringing apps to life. The onboarding supports and develops the ideas of their previous project, Orion. The main thing I like is how they chose the size of the camera and shooting controls: they are fairly large, with meaty strokes. At the same time, restrained. They do not suffer from gigantism. Easy to hit with a finger. In the default iPhone camera, controls demand more dexterity and fine motor skills. A super-clear status: "recording!" check out the record button animation. Controls in settings - lol. The size of a stadium. Cool The landing has no visual revelations. With excellent copywriting
