Want to touch the future? You can literally touch it with your hands and feel the metallic taste. This is a technological demo from Microsoft, where every frame of Quake 2 is generated by an LLM in real time! In a duel with a Strogg, to avoid doom, I used a trick - looked at the ceiling. After that the battle space regenerated and the enemy disappeared 🥲 Perhaps in the future games will still need video cards, but not in the form we are used to. If Sony with PlayStation 6 goes down the PlayStation 3 path - ignoring reality. "We have a super-powerful processor! It will be enough for everything!" Meanwhile the world: the cooler the video card, the cooler the graphics - then interesting times await us: gaming through Sony's eyes is traditional gaming, big consoles. Gaming through Microsoft's eyes is hyper-personalized worlds and thin clients
