Right now, on Wednesday evening, Music has a fairly laconic construction in the lower part of the screen: - one translucent backing, with informer texts and icons on it - the mini-player with its own controls lies as the upper layer Two layers of elements and information-navigation In the demo: our backing, mini-player, tab bar in the new style with its own background and with the active tab background. Four different surfaces instead of the old two Liquid Glass should create a feeling of transparency and lightness of the interface. Coming at us like this, in one leap, does not solve it I spent the day adapting. Apple has its own iOS 26 element library in Figma. Overall, easy game: copy/paste and ⌥+⌘+B - and now we have floating panels, and truly, more content became visible than before.
