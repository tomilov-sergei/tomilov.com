From words to worlds The Sora app from OpenAI is out - a social network where there is only generated content. You can give permission to reuse your own likenesses. You cannot upload likenesses of others On onboarding, there is a lightning-fast "biometrics" assembly. I like how appropriately progressive blur is used in the upper part of onboarding The basis of the app is a feed of vertical videos. My condolences to all the "I told you, you had to add AI, then it would have flown!" TikTok killers, I feel what you feel The difference from TikTok: there is a horizontal pager of remixes of the topic-starter's plot. In my demo, look at the story with a dog stealing meat for steaks (also with Sam stealing video cards) Until copying into Instagram: 3... 2... To fly into the brave new world, after which an embellished Instagram life will seem like slums on the outskirts of a technological conglomerate, you need an invite. I have three invites (gave one to Danya, our generalissimo of AI-things) Everyone who gets into Sora is given four invites. I suggest sharing with each other in the comments.
