Today we are looking at beautiful pictures from the Apple Design Team. Screenshots from interviews with people working on Vision Pro for Wallpaper. It is quite difficult to talk about the work done when you are covered from head to toe with an NDA. What I took from the material: - they worked on the project for more than ten years. - at the start there was no foundation at all: neither technology nor product image. - they prototyped a lot. And most importantly: the approach! Create an environment of comfortable interaction experience. Apple carries its values through the years: design, user experience, emotions during interaction. A complex, deep language, operating with substance that we still have to reach, overcoming all these endless "MVPs" and "we will fix it in the next redesign." You cannot make a first impression twice. I also thought how nicely Arc Search's design combines with the new visual language. How it has already started sprouting into new products on the market. Then I realized that this feeling was helped by Night Shift turned up to the maximum. I made a picture so it is clear how it changes the feeling of screen materials.
