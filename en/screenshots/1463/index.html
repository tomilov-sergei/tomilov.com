Nothing Phone was shown a couple of weeks ago. I want to draw attention to the video Why Phone (3) took so long? It is all about designers' work How intensive the work on the device image was. You want to look at these sketches. In them you can notice how the final form was crafted. This resonates with me because we are now busy with the same thing - searching for a suitable form for our ideas There is also work with references. Something product designers sometimes miss. And this is what allows you to build a solid foundation And I like the continuity of generations through decisions that are not obvious at first glance: a common grid between generations - because of it, lines and modules acquired a common rhythm And how interestingly the designer talks about finding a lightweight embodiment for the upper left corner. Until he said it, I had not thought about it, but indeed - on top models the camera piece looks like a heavy construction that shifts the balance. Thank you for drawing attention to this! More different attention, more quality decisions
