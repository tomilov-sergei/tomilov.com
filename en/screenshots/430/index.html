Wheee! New filters in Instagram. Is this a New Year gift? The company returned to its roots and understood why people fell in love with it? Well then, on the contrary, they should throw everything out and leave only the Hudson filter. This is no New Year, you just do not post content, so you had not seen it. Their blog post from November 15 talks about a whole pack of improvements and additions. What is beautiful: interface design in a thin frame colored with the branded gradient. What is interesting: they introduced content interaction mechanics native to iOS. Here there is "pinch to zoom" and the standard search interface instead of their own custom one. Fewer custom solutions - higher app sustainability. That is, fewer pieces will need to be rewritten and adjusted to operating system updates.
