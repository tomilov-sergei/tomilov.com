There are no temporary The history of computers is living through an interesting period. Enough time has passed, and the formation stories of our industry are emerging. In 1994, the hard drive formatting dialog window was assembled on the knee, simply taking into account all the functions that might be needed in the utility. With the thought that later they would make it well In the 30 years since its creation, the interface has not changed much. Later has still not arrived. Is this the curse of digital systems development or a divine spark? Interfaces designed in "floors" have a margin of strength. See the structure of the Instagram feed. We designed the Zen feed the same way: floors-modules hook to each other, everything is sewn inside, saving time on correcting indents. Need other functions? We dissect the "floor." Or assemble a new docking module. What I put the original tweet into my library for is "temporariness of solutions." I will collect dozens of such debts that are all waiting for their later. Solutions need to be pushed through at the moment of first release. Later it will drown in the backlog and never come
