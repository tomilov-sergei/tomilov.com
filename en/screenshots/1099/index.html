At the end of the year, one should probably sum things up. As they say: Well, it happened and it happened "IT HAPPENED!" I like the scene where the Zvuk icon flows from the phone onto the big screen at the moment the devices touch. I like in the desktop web demo how the weather changes when the mood changes. I like these wild toys in the style of Soviet post-apocalypse or the playgrounds we grew up on. It is funny how a simple desire to make an additional app icon grew into a large-scale icon contest with valuable prizes. The "So, blyat" icon is there at the end of the video. It roughly reflects my feeling of 2024 I like how Zhenya came up with diluting the showreel with footage from live performances. It added life and depth. We once had a brainstorm around O2O. "Online to offline" or "offline to online." I no longer remember which is correct. So this kind of mixing of the bodily and the bodiless is exactly about that. The essence has been captured. Thanks to the whole Zvuk design team and the helping teams for the fact that, thanks to your efforts, materials and reasons for the showreel appeared ✨ The admin's new year begins next week. Nervous
