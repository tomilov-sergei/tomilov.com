Do you stay awake at night so you do not miss a new Apple presentation? Relax already! Apple cooks in its own universe. Safe, toothless, expensive, with every corner rounded: "Never do anything first." Browser Company is one of the most fun teams. A breath of fresh air after a stuffy meeting room. Announcements like this are a joy. By the way, it was shot on a comb and a coat-cleaning roller. With their Arc browser, they make a strong statement on the topic of social interactions. You can share tabs! Spaces! And what? What? No way... Split view can be shared! So I took and shared a split view, check it out And most importantly, all of this is humane. You do not need to think about how it will open for another person. It will open the way you see it. They embedded a browser into the browser. A page opens, styled as a browser with shared tabs. Click the tabs, read right there. Close. We are magnificent! Yes, Safari has had a feature for sharing tab groups for a couple of years already. Do you use it? Exactly!
