How do you take screenshots of working CarPlay interfaces? Take a regular screenshot on the phone by pressing lock and volume up at the same time. 🪄 You are magnificent. In the example, a screenshot from connecting to a rented BMW with a fairly wide panel. It is noticeable that elongated screens were not baked into CarPlay — the part of the panel that belongs more to the passenger than the driver is not used at all. They could have put little buttons there so the co-pilot and, by extension, music manager would have fun too.
