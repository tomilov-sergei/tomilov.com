And what was I doing in 2015? Prototyping the behavior of an operating system and browser for tablets. We were making a system where tabs and running apps live together in the bottom panel. And there was a task: how to return to the home screen from an open state? I did not want to add special buttons to the bottom panel I wanted to create an intuitive, gesture-based interaction experience. People hold tablets by the sides, and a swipe from the edge of the screen would close any app and return to the home screen. In 2017, a similar mechanic would appear in iPhone X In 2015, Framer already existed. In the video you can see that I have a trial. Back then Framer was much more about JS than WYSIWYG
