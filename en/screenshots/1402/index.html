macOS Tahoe 26 Developer Beta Looks very sexy! Clean, fresh. Funny that the glass overlay on the sidebar renders the wallpaper glow even if the window lies on top of another window. Distortion rendering works in real time. This is not baked graphics, but honest realtime. I changed the wallpaper and the background in the screen brightness switcher adapts to changes on the fly (see video) Safari in the first beta is only in one state: tabs as a second row. In stable macOS 15 (old old old) there is an option for compact tabs and controls in one row, I used it. In general, with tabs - a mess. Too much decor by first impressions. Without tabs - a cutie! People on the internet are afraid that the interfaces are low-contrast. There is more contrast than before. They use HDR capabilities to the fullest. This cannot be conveyed in screenshots; the edges of glass buttons are rendered with higher brightness than white, the one that is 255,255,255. More about contrast. I have two screenshots here, Music and Podcasts. You can see that for now different teams style apps differently. Music has black icons. Podcast designers still live in the old world and gray everything The desktop Telegram interface already feels so old-school compared with Messages This is not a revolution, this is evolution. Feels natural. I think a person not deeply immersed in our topics will not notice the changes
