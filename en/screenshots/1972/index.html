The naked, raw process of creativity implies tension and work on both sides, iterations and passes to each other, even if you are passing not to a person Danya Kovchiy talks about his collection of rubber dinosaurs _______ What do we know about good solutions? How do they arrive? Lately I have been deeply involved with these processes: formulating meanings, the birth and maintenance of ideas, searching for criteria for assessing the quality of solutions Insights are born in the pauses between phrases prepared for each other. Not laying them out to each other means closing the door for the new to arrive The new does not come under stress. Stress is greedy - it takes all the space available to it. Both internal and external. Creating a safe environment is an obligatory part of the process The new does not come from emptiness. The universe does not speak; it echoes accumulated material: conversations, opinions, impressions, emotions and feelings An elegant solution usually appears behind a dozen expected, predictable solutions from the lower branch, the near shelf. Without clearing the lower shelf, you cannot climb higher The strangest thing I have started noticing and paying attention to: ideas need to steep. Crystallization of ideas can take days-weeks-months Crystallization happens in silence Give the new space and time. Inside yourself and around
