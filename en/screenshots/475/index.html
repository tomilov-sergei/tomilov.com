Imagine that no hyper-jump happens, and in the future an LLM becomes the same commodity in the OS as spell checking. And here is a curious concept of relations with information in such a future. If we need detail - we pinch. If, on the contrary, we want a short extract - we zoom out. And this way it will be possible to work with any information. At minimum, new horizons for designing responsive sites open here. It will be necessary to keep in mind that for users, any text block is a block of arbitrary size. And it will also be cool in such a future to make a gesture on a thread in a work chat as a signal: give me a short useful extract without the flood Please look at the concept author's site. There are more amusing things there. Thanks to @dtrbn for sharing the concept ✨
