I am a fan of Material You. In the latest betas, the team improved system behavior during the back gesture. If the stack is over, the app will collapse. The app window sticks to the finger and moves with it. It looks and feels so good, as if Apple invented it. Competition is the main partner of innovation. Where it seemed everything had already been invented, there is still room for improvement. In the screencast you can see how the behavior differs between apps that support the behavior and "old" ones. Zvuk does not support it yet, while Spotify already does. How quick they are!
