Predicting the further course of events is not difficult Apple, already lagging in the AI race, continues to cling to the outdated concept…

Predicting the further course of events is not difficult Apple, already lagging in the AI race, continues to cling to the outdated concept of so-called "reality", by inertia investing in "analogness", "tactility" and "live emotions". This pushes their products ever more toward the high-margin luxury segment. The iPhone becomes something like a Porsche 911 for millennials with a midlife crisis who managed to earn money in the fat years The bet on "reality" leads to desynchronization with reality. In the new economic and political conditions, the corporation's value paradigm finds no response and is perceived by some as an atavism, by others as mockery Zoomers and Alpha, getting protein from processed insects in underground shelters under cloud multithreaded neural slop, cannot afford reality even with a mortgage, which over a six-year horizon leads Apple to a sharp fall in revenue, an inability to invest in R&D, degradation, and the death of the company
