Daylight Computer stopped being a teaser-placeholder and became a promo. It is hard to imagine a more appropriate place for the trendy technique of "shadows of the surrounding world in the background." How organically it fits into the product concept by meaning 🌞 I like how this technique moves around, fighting monotony: the site's background - graphic design of little cookie-pictures about the operating system. And for dessert: a 60fps demo of the device in action. When, in developing materials, did you go beyond the great-grandfathers' "24 frames per second is enough for everyone"?
