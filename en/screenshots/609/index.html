Beautiful-beauty concept of a player for a locked phone screen. In the comments, the author explains that the first and second pictures are originally the standard square track cover. Then a little hand magic and a sexy result ✨ I do not like the aesthetic with this mechanistic outline of controls and blocks. Because of the outline, interfaces often become noisier, line rattling appears. An amazing property: it seems you add one property to an object, but in reality you complicate the construction. The author probably wanted to achieve a feeling like in visionOS, but was inattentive.
