My own canvas instead of a Miro embed. Once I played around with Framer until tomilov.com appeared. Screenshot 1 Sometimes I gave talks here and there, so I made an archive containing all the materials. The animation lectures are still relevant — take a look. They are free. I came up with a visualization called “What’s in My Head” as the face of the site. I gathered some material on a Miro board. The end. I was too lazy to prepare materials separately and keep the board current. Then I became too lazy to keep paying for Miro. The sense of purpose disappeared. Then paying for Framer stopped being interesting too — here is the post about moving to HTML with Codex. June 17, 2026 — I ask Codex to build a canvas for me. Screenshot 2 I think about how to keep it current: my posts are the material for “what’s in my head.” The fresher a post, the closer it sits to the center; older ones move toward the edge of the map. An hour of Codex work, and there is a canvas where posts are grouped by related topics and extend in rays beyond the horizon, every post clickable. Screenshot 3 The feeling is like opening Paintbrush in Windows 3.11 for the first time — my God, I can do absolutely anything! What an astonishing time to be alive. It gives me goosebumps.
