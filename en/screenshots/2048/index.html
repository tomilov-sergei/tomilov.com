The ChatGPT app update has a beautiful example of the Continuity principle. Or, put differently: an embodiment of the Liquid Glass design principles — I wrote about it here. The add control, the plus button, smoothly unfolds into a menu of options. Unexpectedly, the camera unfolds just as continuously, without taking you away from the current context. Bravo! The principle of continuity in interface interaction: Visual connectedness; uninterrupted interaction with the app. Every object lives in one space that transforms under your finger. The eye and brain understand where these clusters of information come from and where they go. Their hierarchy is clear.
