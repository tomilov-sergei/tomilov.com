Sometimes they come back Remember Dot? A personal assistant that understands context, an empathetic companion for life. Let us remember it The new project has entered beta testing. It is Genie AI geniegetsme.com - a personal assistant, empathetic, context-understanding blah blah blah, now with a taste of sociality I connect them with the late Dot because of a striking similarity of family traits. Which have evolved • Now the UI, in its trend, gravitates toward the formalism of the ChatGPT UI • Experiments with gesture control - closing opened entities with a swipe from the bottom of the screen to the middle - sexy. A little bit like Xzibit: we put iOS inside iOS so you can close while you close. In the second beta, the swipe area was enlarged and this gesture works more conveniently than in Safari in iOS 26 • And most importantly! What delights me: they are using shaders everywhere. The effects in the showreel are not from After Effects, but from the app! Chromatic aberration effects during swipes, geometric distortions of the image during scrolling and opening-closing "windows" Conclusion: need to master shaders - they give the next level of immersion in interaction with an app
