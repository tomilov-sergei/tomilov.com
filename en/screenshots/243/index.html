Recently we discussed how the new iPhone camera presents information. Back then I formulated it for myself like this: "Is the move away from simplification, the transition to the language usually spoken by photo equipment, a blurring (loss) of values or a conscious turn in development?" Andrey carefully noted that it was too early to draw conclusions, more data needed to be collected. I went and collected it. Looks like we can conclude that this is conscious positioning of the Pro models. On Pro models they show focal length. On regular models: simple zoom values. I wonder what Google Pixel has there? I will look tomorrow.
