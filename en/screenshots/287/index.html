Popup as art I experience a strange pleasure of looking. Yes, this is basic support for light and dark themes. And it is sexy. Accent red is equally good here and there. Are there many services that are not afraid to use red as the main color? An interesting effect from the combination of the picture and the background. On the light theme, the popup feels like a single space with the picture, thanks to the light surface on which the plate stands. And the black "close" cross rhymes with the graphic strokes. On the dark theme, these strokes on the corner of the plate and the heart already work toward a harmonious combination with the black background of the message. Beautiful this way, and beautiful that way! Did the black theme need a white cross? 🌚
