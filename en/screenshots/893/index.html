Note or note? - Select the needed text - Choose "Share" in the context menu - Find ourselves at a crossroads: 1. We can create a note 2. We can create a quick note In the first case, we get the needed text transferred without preserving context. In the second case, a link to the email, but without the needed text. Why so? What, two different teams are making one product and not talking to each other? Mmm, what a familiar taste of pain. Wait-wait! If you create a quick note through Control Center, an option appears to add note context. So here we have three teams that do not talk to each other. "Well, you understand, yes, we agree, we also want it to be consistent, are you sure about the quality of the solution, did you test it on users? Fine, we will add it in Q5. Will Q5 work for you?" Now it becomes truly painful. There is a feeling that the project had an ideologist, George, with a clear vision of how to take interaction with Notes to a new level. But George got promoted, shifted his focus somewhere, and his whole vision blurred in streams of meetings and discussions 😒 We were just discussing-discussing that people can be helped by labeling the "esc" and "↵" keys, and among five people this knowledge got lost. What can one say about three teams! And what a revolutionary product we could have had if George had not gone for a promotion
