There is a feeling that LLMs from under the wing of search services and those born without a long search legacy went to different universities. Pictures in order: - ChatGPT and Perplexity - we say what we know. - Alice and Gemini - it is not that simple. Let us pour you some semantic fluff, just in case. And values from search results have also sprouted in us: information density and technical details. In terms of layout beauty, Perplexity is clearly looking at ChatGPT, but falls a little short in elegance. Excessive flicker from small specks makes the whole presentation rattle. In this regard, I want Alice to step away from the desire to fit more onto one screen, relax, and stop making everything so tiny. ChatGPT has pleasant typography. Here I want to raise the question of "brand perception vs organic perception." I have a hypothesis that implementing branded typefaces interferes with the feeling of a "native app" 🤔
