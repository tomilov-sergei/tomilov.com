Floor796 Floor796 is an animated scene showing the lives of characters from various works on the 796th floor of a huge space station. The animation is regularly expanded with new blocks (rooms) and characters from movies, TV series, games, anime, memes, etc. The project is being created by one author as a hobby starting in 2018. An addictive project: almost every character in this diorama can be clicked and you can admire the source A grand piece of work. The site has an update log.
