Pictures of the new Nothing Phone. We do not know exactly which one. There is speculation that it is their next flagship, and an opinion that it is a more democratic version - because of the red cube on the back panel. Allegedly the red cube is a marker of more accessible versions. If so, that is beautiful. For example, Levi's has Red Line. A line of cheaper jeans. The phone pictures are beautiful. The details teased by the official Twitter account resemble what is in the pictures. I like how they do not leave camera representations alone. Fresh. Unusual. Unfamiliar. It excites I keep one eye on what Nothing is doing. From a clearly expressed cyberpunk narrative, their universe is becoming more conformist. And I do not think this is about compromise. After all, the first phones existed, so there is courage. Perhaps this is about internal feelings from the device. In an era of hyperstimulation, an additional disco starts to tire. A good, clear case showing that feeling for the balance between aesthetics and decorativeness is big, complex work that deserves respect. Not every year do projects appear that brightly draw their own magical worlds
