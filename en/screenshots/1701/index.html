Instagram is gradually rolling out an update. Not as radical as the universal reels-like feeds were. They changed the navigation The add-content button moved from the tab bar to the top left corner. Messages moved into the tab bar and lost their exclusive position Reels became the second tab, shifting the discovery scenario two points to the right The logo moved to the central part of the header, shifting the balance and arrangement There was probably a lot of homework done under the hood - now you can switch between tabs with swipes And brand illustrations are being forced more strongly and breaking further into the product What a game! Ah, what a game!
