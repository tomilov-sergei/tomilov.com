Recently we discussed Books and the return of the authentic page-turning mechanism. And here is an application in an unexpected place. Retro plans to guide people through onboarding with this mechanic. Like a paper manual for a freshly bought box with discs (or even floppies). How does this guy from Perm know what software boxes are? The maximum we had was localized licensed discs with Warcraft III. At the turn of the eras I managed to work as an expert on matters related to computing equipment. A helpdesk guy. And I sold boxes with Kaspersky. The boxes looked solid, people paid a thousand rubles for them. We need to invent how to bring the experience of owning a boxed version into our time. Like this experience of flipping through a manual.
