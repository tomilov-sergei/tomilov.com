NASA's Curiosity Mars rover used its Mast Camera, or Mastcam, to capture this view of Earth setting while Phobos, one of Mars' two moons, is rising. It's the first time an image of the two celestial bodies have been captured together from the surface of Mars. How often does a man think about Phobos? And about Tatooine? And about a lonely rover roaming the expanses of a planet so distant and at the same time so close to us, like an office worker moving between rooms in search of the meaning of what is happening?
