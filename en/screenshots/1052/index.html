A beautiful landing page, mainfra.me. It also works with nostalgia for "those very" times when computers were big and monitors were semi-round. Interesting that the way of exploring and understanding new technologies rests on previous experience. "We got computers and the world changed" = "We got LLMs and the world is changing." Does it work? It seems it works. We always need some clear, vivid metaphor. I like this phrase: CD, RM, PING: COMMANDS WE MEMORIZED TO TRANSLATE OUR DESIRES INTO ACTIONS... IF WE CHOSE TO LEARN THEM. It clearly refers us to learning prompting over the last couple of years. This is clearly a dead-end branch. But you cannot jump over a step. It must be passed through. People from the industry will pass through it, integrate it, then, if we continue the landing authors' metaphors, synthesize something similar to Windows, where there will be Solitaire and Paintbrush. And then it will become understandable to a broader audience. For now, all our GPTs remain the domain of geeks: cd c:/games/doom
