And what kind of rabbit hole is this? robot.co is a project by Dashiellsem Ponce de Leon LinkedIn Dash, aka @creativedash, is the co-founder of UI8 and founder of Creativedash The grids from the previous post are a building block of the future platform. I asked for an invite and got inside It looks like a social network for creators: • social components: there is an activity dashboard like on GitHub, a chat, and a personal "wall" • the creator part - fields for experiments. Most of it is in coming soon status. You can upload your own files for use in projects What is interesting from a designer's position: the project feels alive. Besides microanimations, support for animated images, and its own style (web 2.0, yes yes), the totality of visual decisions gives the interface a feeling of light tactility. A pleasant feeling that is inviting and gives a sense of safety The model account in Robot is the Robot profile: https://robot.co/robot Dash himself is more modest: https://robot.co/dash follow me on robot.co, together we will watch what this grows into.
