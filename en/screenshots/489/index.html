It looks like a fat period has been put on the bento-design trend. The Amie calendar team produced a very bright statement. I talked about their product recently. Admiring the landing page I like this crazy infinite scroll. It is interesting to dive in and examine the decisions. And if you look from a bird's-eye view, the visual language is clearly visible. And, um, I want to call this visual language: Apple Visual Design Language. Everything here seems assembled from native iOS and macOS components and solutions from the Apple site. Can we say the team did not spend effort on "inventing" an image, but focused on the essence of the product? Kind of like Telegram. I see a trend not toward a pretentious "branded" appearance, but toward mimicry of a standard native app, in order to painlessly replace standard apps with their "improved" versions By the way, the animations are made in Rive ^^
