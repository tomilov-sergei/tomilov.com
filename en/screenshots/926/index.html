Notion suggests turning on an assistant. Or a new assistant. I cannot say how much better this new AI is than that new one that was there before. Honestly, I do not really follow the evolution of species inside their universe. What I liked: - "credits" running across the screen as a way to hint at what the assistant can do - The image of the little dude. It is consistent with the product's graphic style and evokes a certain empathy in me. Somehow I even wanted to say something to it. But the previous new AI assistant, which looked like three stars, lacked this empathetic layer Yes, the little dude looks like another little dude. Maybe they are brothers. It is curious: when all AI assistants being created now end up in a single noosphere, will they face the same complicated introduction to each other as human subpersonalities do?
