We have a letter! That very one, to that very place. Today, screenshots from Harry Potter: Magic Awakened. The project feels like a second screen for Hogwarts Legacy. It is not. I am trying to feel out the right epithet for this quality, craft execution. The animated style allows it not to fight for realistic graphics in an environment with limited resources — an old find, since Diablo III. Since those same times, it has shown that investment in art will help a game keep looking current for many years. This drawing style is to my taste. Probably the overdose from the latest Spider-Man is showing. The screenshot has a pleasant immersive detail: we put the signature where it should be placed. And the signature of the person who sent the letter is right there nearby, so nobody misses that the letter is from that very cat-woman herself.
