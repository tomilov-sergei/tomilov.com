Edits is out. A separate video editor app from the Instagram team. Interesting that the aesthetic is not Instagram-like, more infantile. Different icons, a different degree of softness create another product image. The brand's signature color coding is in place. And the addition of yellow, the technical creative color from the Apple universe, looks appropriate and native. I like the funny rhyme in the tabs between the square sticker control and the plus. And there is also the ability to work with HDR content. Or not work with it. The option can be turned off and the device clearly shows the difference! If only we could learn to work with HDR content in our product 😇
