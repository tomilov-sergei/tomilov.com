A computer inside a computer Trying a new work setup for myself. Arrange life so that the personal noosphere and the work one do not intersect, but move in parallel universes. Telegram for me is 99% work questions. Communication with family and close ones is in Messages. Work projects in Drive, personal ones in iCloud and so on. The problem is with the browser. And with the plague of our century: browser tabs. They multiply faster than cringe threads on Twitter! It is hard to separate the "home" window to the internet and the work one. On Andrey's tip, I tried OpenIn - did not manage it. The obvious solution: use different browsers. For home and for work. The problem is that links come from completely unpredictable channels: from the calendar, PDF documents, and of course from Telegram. Which breaks everything. Links open in "home" Safari. In short, TG broke everything. I figured out here how to integrate it into the concept of two browsers. Open the web version! Arc's built-in split mode turns the browser into an operating system. Busta Rhymes is my brother from another mother.
