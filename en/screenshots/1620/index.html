Hero today Remind Faster This is a "thin client" for Apple's standard Reminder. The goal of the app: optimize the process of creating a reminder. A small app with a cluster of small pleasant interface and product finds Suggestions for the day and time of the reminder: pay attention to the 09:00 time item and the arrow above - an indicator of the next day. Or "+1h" - any entered time can be quickly shifted one hour forward The monetization mechanics impress. They do not jump out during interaction in any way. They wait for their time in the app settings. And they are visual! There is a preview of what a person will get by activating the upgrade. "What a small app, in the spirit of modern trends!" I thought. And the small app has been developing since 2015. Thank you, Nick, for developing it!
