What music does Sam Altman listen to? What about Adam Mosseri? Dylan Field? Panama Playlists This is a site assembled by an anonymous person with a selection of playlists from famous people. It claims to collect data based on people having the playlist privacy option open. The person searched for data not only in Spotify, but also in Pandora, for example, which is indicated in the corresponding playlists I like three things in this story: 1. How cool brand attributes work. The page directly says that Spotify is not involved in the project. But the feeling is the exact opposite! 2. A cool project with playlist selections. A new way to find music through these, also quite real, influencers 3. Nicely laid out, modesty nudges toward use. Curious what it would look like if Spotify itself took on the project
