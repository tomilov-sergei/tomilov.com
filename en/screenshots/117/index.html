My iPad stopped being "my iPad" quite a long time ago; it is a cinema where all episodes of Stillwater run endlessly. I recommend the series. Turns out you can create interesting cartoons where there is no stereotypical link from the cartoons of our childhood — action=violence. All the experienced emotions and life situations in the series are familiar to us from childhood; we of course lived through all of it. In short, my focus on iPad software drifted away. Thanks to Nastya for the comment on the previous post about Books on the iPad. All the same things from yesterday's conversation. Like on the mobile phone screen, only unfolding more fully. Book previews in the store open as a carousel and you can see the content of the neighboring book. The page-turning simulation pretends the book has a binding. Now I have the feeling that I was using a demo version of a full product. Books feels like an iPad-first product. Is there much similar software? By the way, Nastya runs a channel where she shares impressions of books she has read.
