Do you have an invite to Invites? You do not need an invite to Invites. The new Apple app is available to everyone. I like the super-smooth intro into the app. Look at the delights of SwiftUI effects. With neat animations and example styling, the app creates a feeling of anticipation - it is going to be fun here. And it hints at what types of events can exist, to remove the feeling of frustration. To light a spark of creativity. The whole inviter, from the beginning to the end of the flow, feels like a game. Although inside it is regular iOS design code: - Backgrounds with emoji patterns. I liked the collage technique - it is unusual to see emoji of different sizes in one composition. Looks fresh enough - Here you can also generate a background with Playground - Immediately add a playlist and give guests access to it - let them throw in their tracks - I want to note the part of the flow with sharing the event. A tiny event preview makes this piece of the flow more emotional too - And separately I want to note that the people who made the app created that very elusive feeling of "they understand me." All they had to do was add a "+1" option Though not all of the design code is ordinary. The top navigation and dialog buttons are larger than usual. This adds a feeling of simplicity Come to my event! We will sit together and keep quiet 🌚
