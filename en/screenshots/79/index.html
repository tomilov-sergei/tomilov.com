We are often asked (once) what apps the editorial team knows: "music for and so on..." Sharing our finds. And you share yours in the comments to the post. 1. Music for luxurious life. How else to describe this style? Turn on Poolsuite FM and your hair becomes smooth and silky. 2. There is a large family of apps for "music for sleep/insomnia/calming the mind." We singled out Pzizz purely because of J.K. Rowling's review of the app. 3. Mubert is for when you are allergic to hype and want to try another style. Endel, you beauties! But it is so good when there is competition. The lazy channel editor wins. 4. My favorite podcast app. "You do not have to do anything" — everything is already prepared for listening under a workout. 5. Our favorite is Odio. Music for everything. A soundtrack for a 12-hour adventure, for contemplating the construction workers across the street from the window. "Tones — joon kwak" — favorite wire hum listened to for 10,000 hours.
