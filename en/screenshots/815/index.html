- Will there be concepts every day? - It would be nice. But no I like the Weather app in the upcoming watchOS 11. For its consistent behavior. Weather, Temperature, Precipitation, Wind, UV Index, Visibility, Humidity, and Air Quality - eight different informers packed into stylistically similar forms. And pay attention to the pager control on the right side of the screen. The third, final state of the app contains much more information and does not fit on one screen. The pager turns into a scrollbar. Pager + Scroll Bar = Scrollger, it turns out 🤔
