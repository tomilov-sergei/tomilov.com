Flighty updated. Now you can add friends and family and share your flights. Again we see memoji in onboarding. Is this the real state of things or a designer's wet fantasy? I am curious how deeply people are involved in the Apple universe and play this game. Until today, I mostly thought that using memoji in third-party services was a nontrivial task. If you look at it through a user's eyes. Turns out everything is quite easy. Flighty can pull the avatar from contact data. And overall, it is such an elegant idea — ask a person to attach their own contact so you can take their avatar and other data if your product needs it. Do you have your own memoji?
