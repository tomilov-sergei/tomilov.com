The main messenger of millennials, Instagram, has themes for chats. Do you use them? How to find them: long-tap on empty chat space opens a sheet with a Charli xcx album set of themes. The themes are pretty, they have pleasant smooth animated backgrounds, and the combinations of message bubbles are harmoniously selected for them. I like how, when you pull the sheet and follow the movement of the view, the size of the background image changes. It feels as if the view reacts sensitively to what is happening. Just like it is alive! Unlike its owner
