Anton Repponen People Look at Art or Art Looks at People Over the years, I’ve noticed I’ve taken a lot of photographs of people looking at art in museums and galleries around the world. I believe there’s something special in those moments—when we observe art, we see something that inspires us, something unfamiliar, or something that quietly resonates. I really like how authentically zoom-out works in this project. Zoom-out is rarely used when scrolling pages because it feels unnatural. The expected content behavior is approach or another friendly interaction. In this project it is done against the principles, and it is appropriate and organic And how the shadow effects from viewers are integrated. Realistic shadows were last year's trend. But this is the first time (second) I see them working not only as decoration, but as part of the narrative. A true visual narrative of entering a gallery!
