Nikita himself made a wonderful, exhaustive demo of searching related images in Google. I have nothing to add. A copy feature of similar search in Pinterest. The graphic image is the same: the icon and the working principle itself. Thanks to the Pinterest team for our happy future. The most interesting thing in today's review is Ellie's jacket, which she took from John Rambo. I stress-tested it. As we can see, it did not recognize Kanye's jacket. I will make a timid assumption that this is Yeezy-Gap-Balenciaga. Looks like. Google is preparing us for the fact that the Australian will become the successor of Rick Owen's corporation. Otherwise, why does he appear in results so often. The search itself handles it well. A funny jump from sandals of the 2018 collection to bananciagas. The collection is one of my favorites. Search by a photo taken with the camera works only in the app. A pity. On the third try, the tappod was recognized. Whose success this is, the one asking the query or the one answering, remains an open question. Once Andrey Los said: at some point search will become a commodity. A person seeing any search field will expect it to answer any question. Some universal access to magical knowledge from the internet. It is curious how different technology companies work through the scenario of "search similar by image." They keep the product purchase scenario in mind. And through joint efforts they create this universal search scenario. The singularity is approaching
