Zerocam A one-button (two-button) app for getting eco-friendly shots. You stop using the power of your new iPhone 16 Pro Max Plus, save battery resource and the processor resources that usually go into digital photography. You get a "simple" shot, exactly the way God intended: "the lens sees." I like how the labels on the button change with every shot. Such kawaii. And I like the branding. The icon, the yellow stretched button, the stretched letter o (well, you get it). A small app made with love ✨ Say: Che-e-e-e-modan
