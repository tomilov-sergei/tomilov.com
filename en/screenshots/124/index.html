How We Feel beautifully violates the iOS guidelines. A notification on a black background, when it is recommended to color closer objects lighter. The team focused only on the dark theme and does it skillfully. You can violate rules when you understand the structure of the principles you are going to bypass. A good exercise: first think and assemble without violating the principles, and only after that do it the way you wanted. In the process, unexplored territories will surface and the final solution will become better. Workshop idea! "Assembling your product on native components." Sign up 💅
