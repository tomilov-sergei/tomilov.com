I copy a phone number; this is probably the second conscious call this year. The first was to IKEA to return an extra mattress. Do not ask. At the moment of receiving the phone number, a chain of the scenario is already building in my head: find the Phone app, somehow paste the number there; I even remember that this is done stupidly non-obviously: you need to long-tap the empty dialer screen. The chain of thoughts is interrupted at the moment search opens. The phone is already here! Want to - call, Want to - write. Want to - create a shortcut Sexy! Someone thought about this. Why? When? What was the person doing at that moment? Who and how decided that the operating system should look into the clipboard and search for phone numbers there? Was this a task in Jira? I feel this is beyond 9 to 5. This is passion and obsession. What can we make simpler? What can we improve, tune up? Every grain-answer is a contribution to a lovable product. Do we burn for our work the same way this person did?
