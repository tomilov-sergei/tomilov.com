In iOS 17.3, the Music app got the ability to create and fill shared playlists. - Inside, the people who added a track are marked. - You can put emoji reactions on individual tracks, but not on the whole playlist. - There are templates for the playlist cover. Interesting solution: the active cover is the one standing in the center of the screen. There is no additional highlighting technique. Apparently because it is not in the iOS design system. - There is even a QR code for simple connection to the playlist. And this is not some internal proprietary analogue, but a regular one.
