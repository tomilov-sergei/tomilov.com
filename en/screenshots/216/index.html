And today's screenshot of the day is a giant from whom I have been waiting for this my whole life! Of course — I am a dark-theme fan, and here we have an app of first necessity, used daily. To enable it, you need to go into settings and change a checkbox. By default it is off. Although why is it off? Look at the CJM: dark theme on the phone is enabled manually, chosen at the first start of the phone after purchase. My point is that the person who manually said "I want dark theme in the OS" will be okay with the app interface being dark. Regardless of platform, most apps inherit OS properties. Expected behavior. I understand. When you sit in a meeting room and make decisions, the behavior does not seem expected. You are afraid the dashboards will change their dynamics. Like: we made dark theme, it is an industry standard, yes. People saw the changed app, realized they did not need the service, the wife has long been looking at Oleg with sparks in her eyes and not at you, the children, flowers of life, demand more and more, you should not have skipped classes at university — now you would have been, wow, already something, the job is unloved, you wanted to play in a rock band your whole life anyway, and those dashboards too... Black dark theme is cool. Turning it on immediately for people who have dark as the default is fine. If anyone from Go suddenly reads me, tell me why you did not enable it immediately.
