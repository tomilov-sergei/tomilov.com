Got access to Amie. It is a calendar with a to-do list. It made noise a couple of months ago with its approach to interface construction. The team suggests working in split-screen mode. The screen is pliable; with a gesture you can unfold one entity or the other to full screen. Super-pleasant smooth animations of expanding and collapsing entities. Inside they offer integration with fitness and health. Health passes sleep data and the calendar draws a beautiful vertical strip with a starry sky. Fitness showed me nothing 😢 In settings there is also a luxurious screen for setting the default meeting duration. I like both the preview occupying a third of the screen and the wide, convenient slider. I make all my default meetings 45 minutes. This year I plan to apply new approaches at work and make meetings 40 minutes. Such a brave guy! Maybe I tangentially touched a whole genre: alternative calendars. A pity I cannot go deeper into it. My work calendar is currently in Yandex. God, we tried to use VK for several months and it was such a disappointment. How has a corporation where mail was the locomotive all these years not made a normal product? And my home calendar lives in iCloud.
