I saw at Vlad's that they made an app called MEL, meant to help establish contact with other people in different situations. Vlad is very interested in this topic. It is so pleasant to see that movement and immersion in the topic of interpersonal communication continues. I like how familiar interface elements and characteristic decorativeness of themes combine with each other. I liked the way they work with patterns: on the main screen they are locked in card frames, and inside a topic they are freed and occupy the whole space. Looks fashionable, yet friendly. Apps often suffer from turning into an art object and distancing themselves from the user. You can be both arty and friendly at the same time. You really want to answer the questions. I want an input field, let the app interview me!
