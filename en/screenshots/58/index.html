Maybe we can already say that kawaii is a cultural phenomenon that erased boundaries and influenced an industry that at first glance is not obvious — applied UI. I am glad about that. Interfaces are created to transmit emotional signals. Here is Any Distance and their cute little cat. By the way, they won an Apple Design Award in the Visuals and Graphics category. Winners in this category feature stunning imagery, skillfully drawn interfaces, and high-quality animations that lend to a distinctive and cohesive theme. And meanwhile we are still arguing: is the absence of UI animations a bug or an optional product property?
