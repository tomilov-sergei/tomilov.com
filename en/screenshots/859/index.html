Apple Intelligence is slowly pulling itself into iOS 18.1 beta This is how extracting the main point in mail works. What is interesting: - It removes the "garbage" text of the email and packs the meaningful part so that it fits into a short push message. In my case: the item and the date when it will be delivered - the Summarize icon. While the world of designers and IT is stewing in its own juice and thinking that three stars* are the new industry standard for denoting the work of LLM magic, Apple, through its own optics - people first - makes understandable markers. At the center is not abstract magic, with which the IT world is currently enchanted, but simple packages people can understand: "here are a couple of lines and the result." - And most importantly! Watch the video. I am delighted by such an elegant UI solution. No promo, no hints, nothing distracts you from ordinary consumption of your ordinary scenarios (hello, Yandex Go). If you want, you will make a short extract of the email yourself. The interface hints at this slightly. The email is shifted a few pixels lower, the brain understands that you can scroll upward. A new, calm, non-obligatory signal in a familiar environment. - And summarize also does not break the body of the email, does not cover interfaces. It lies neatly above. Hinting that now extracts will become primary.
