Animated covers in Apple Music and Spotify live in different universes. I would like them to live in one, of course. Then maybe all streaming services could receive this animated content from suppliers "as change." It looks impressive; when there is rich visual content, you want to experiment with presentation. Notice how Apple has a pleasant, gradual blur closer to the bottom of the screen, a smooth transition into the player. Apple made new APIs: visualEffect API. These things have become not difficult at all. In the previous version of Zen, we called it Zen 2.0, we had the same techniques for smoothly transitioning the article image into the text snippet. Back then we ate five dogs on it — polishing the implementation mechanisms of this brave design thought. (More on that tomorrow)
