I am a fan of the map in Snapchat. It is a quick way to see where the action is right now. A way to discover interesting spots and look at the surroundings through other people's eyes. Why are these scenarios hidden so deeply in Instagram? Venues run their accounts, people share stories. You could explore the surroundings the same way. The possibilities exist, but the way to reach them is not obvious. Or it is obvious and I am just a boomer? For boomers they could draw a little globe in the corner of the Discovery tab, so a tap would summon this rich geo-Instagram.
