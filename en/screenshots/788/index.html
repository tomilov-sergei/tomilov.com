The healthy-person's hamster, Loop Hero, came out on mobile platforms It is an indirect control game from a Russian studio. The hamster knight runs by himself; we only change his equipment and set up the environment. It is a roguelike where our role is that of a kind of game master. It is good, play it. You can also listen to a podcast episode about development. The game's designer came to "How Games Are Made." Honestly, the pixel-art aesthetic has never attracted me. I am closer to quality drawing like in Hollow Knight. What to pay attention to here: - The high-quality work with contrast. A very primitive playing field and a conditional image of the hero are accompanied by very pleasant, detailed authorial drawings of heroes and antagonists.
