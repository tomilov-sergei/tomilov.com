Offsuit An app for playing poker. Probably the most kawaii and stylish one on the market. What I especially liked: other players' avatars. Designers often draw Memoji avatars or beautiful photos in layouts. There are even separate photos of people that became memes and move between concepts on Dribbble. In reality, God willing, 10% of people in a service have them. That is, in production it is hard to get a picture like in the layout. There will be placeholders instead of beautiful avatars. Here they solved it elegantly: you must choose from a preset. ♣️♠️
