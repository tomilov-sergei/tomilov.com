Warcraft is out! But not that one... Warcraft Rumble is tower offense. Practically strategy. There is a base, we buy units. What is cool: there is a single-player "campaign," we open different locations, rejoice at familiar faces. I played a little; the feeling is as if they gave me a teaser of that very game. Beautiful assets, quality models, favorite sounds. At minimum, you can spend a couple of evenings nostalgizing. I want to note that the game is designed for vertical orientation and plays and feels excellent in this format. I can imagine playing a full Warcraft III remake in this form. We would be running a Lost Temple remake right now 🤌 There is another view of the release too. A deep analysis of the game's mechanics from Deconstructor of Fun. With graphs, comparisons of the meta with other battle royales, a deep dive into monetization and progression. The fact that the meta system feels different is refreshing and exciting.
