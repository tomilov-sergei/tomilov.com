Today, in one conversation that happened, the well-known-to-many Flo was mentioned "I used it, but at some point it had so many features and insistent calls to try-enable all of them. I switched to the built-in iOS one" Can the extensive path of product development be called the Evernote effect? And write a book: watch the critical mass of features. Most likely you are not flying because of their number. In the inertia of the main driving force, there is a high probability of not noticing all the entropy that will become ballast and drag you to the bottom. Product redesign is the main way to drop ballast. Questions to yourself before sleep: "Am I not creating ballast?" "Can a product have a finished sufficient number of features?" "How do you grow a business if you do not touch the product's 'ideal state'?"
