I saw a concept of improved in-app notifications on Twitter. And I kind of cannot with this. Pushes appeared in 2008! Just take it and in 2025 move the frame wherever you want. Why inside your own universe, and internal pushes are decisions on the app side, hold on to limitations invented by other people for other purposes? Because the designer does not feel like the owner of the space they work with. Does not question existing decisions and patterns. Well ok. At least look not at 2008, but at 2022. The year Dynamic Island appeared. You can already hook onto a more functional and broader creative frame. And if you also look at how design can be made for it, you can get inspired and take a couple of steps into 2025, or even 2026!
