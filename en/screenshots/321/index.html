A line about an Android user's lime-green messages Nothing is trying to solve the task of the millennium. To create conditions under which users of their products will not be kicked out of group chats. In the past iMessage, now simply Messages, is wildly popular in the States. People there correspond more only in Snapchat. When a person without iMessage enters a group chat, it breaks. It turns into Stone Age SMS/MMS correspondence. No party with sparkles, crazy reactions, and secret photos for you. Why do they need this? I think this is primarily for KOLs. Opinion leaders. A modern hip-hop performer is much more personal media than creativity. It is a character manifested on Twitter, an image on Instagram, clothing choice matters. In 2023, if you are not in Rick, you are not a rapper. Image is the driving force. And creativity is more like a party. You with a crowd of your guys, bring your girlfriends. There are so many names on albums, on feats, that they will not fit into one tweet. Parties do not stop. They are constantly calling each other, texting. Posting screenshots of conversations on social networks. And there are blue bubbles. You have no blue bubbles, you have no rap. For people who bought their phone, Nothing spins up virtual Mac minis where true Messages will run. Blue bubbles for lovers of cyberpunk aesthetics. This is a design solution. This is cool
