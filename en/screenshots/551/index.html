I am still afraid of suggestions. It is impossible to predict what will happen when I tap the text. What will happen when I tap the words "apple vision pro"? I expect the thing to go into the input line, like keyboard suggestion words go into the input line when I am typing these lines. Lol. No. Designers decided that the experience of typing text should be ignored. After all, their scenario is the most frequent in a person's life - they will relearn! Tapping the words will throw me straight to results, but I want to add a couple more words. I need to make extra clicks In Arc Search, suggestion options can be scrolled into the input line and you can continue adding to the query. This behavior should become an industry standard! It is so convenient. It feels intuitive and does not mislead. Please, let us make this suggestion behavior everywhere. Let us make the world just a little more convenient
