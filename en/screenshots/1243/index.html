For some it does not work, for some it works In my view, this does not decrease value, it increases it. This is recognition of a contribution to world culture. This is recognition of authorship. Without the original author, without his vision, without the cultural layer formed by this author - there is no value. No one will stylize pictures after you if no one knows you. The more people know about you, the more people will go watch your next film. "Princess Mononoke", released in IMAX last Thursday, earned $4 million. Win-Win What is bad about people using a bright, recognizable style for their own purposes?
