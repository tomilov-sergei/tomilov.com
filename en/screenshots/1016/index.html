Suno - Midjourney for music It seems like a rare case where they jumped onto fresh trends and it worked. The team manages to assemble a whole, through-line image from communication to product. Noisy gradients, serif type, sepia in photos - they work as if in counterphase to the generative glossy pictures of generated tracks inside the platform. They create contrast and give a clue to where the platform's content is, and where its users' content is. A bold story: take a Dribbble trend and make it your main one. If Suno survives, these techniques will become an industry standard, like Ugly Shoes thanks to Balenciaga, which others picked up and it turned into everyday life. Balenciaga were not the first. Nor is Suno. The small guys at Lux.Camera launched Orion on the same trend P.S. Check out the track. On a scale from 0 to 10, how Wow is this? Let us quote the classics: "Well, to the leather musicians and performers. Without clinking glasses"
