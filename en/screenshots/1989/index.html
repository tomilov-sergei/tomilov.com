I spent a little time thinking: "How does this land for me?" I wildly liked one thing: the system knows there is a passport photo, and when you need to enter the data in the browser, it pulls it in - that is real design. 10 out of 10. But the feature is too small for such loud claims about reinventing the platform. My skepticism is reinforced by memories of past Google events; about 6 years have already passed since they showed intelligent features that never saw the light During and after watching, the main thought kept revolving around an external axis How much the Android team has to contort itself to win audience back from Apple. It looks like they no longer play the game of a distinctive visual language, but play the FMCG game: "We are the same, inside the bottle is the same shampoo - choose by the label". Engineers learned to make progressive blur and blur What is good for design - you can make more consistent things while relying less on platform frames Bottom line - so far it does not really do anything for me (5 out of 10)
