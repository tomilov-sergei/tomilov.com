And this is it! In the latest Telegram update, the team says they made a new visual effect for deleting messages, "Thanos Snap" 🫰 Watching in slow motion, you can see that not only the background falls apart into particles; the effect carefully disassembles each letter individually. Interesting how it is implemented. They emphasize that this is an energy-saving implementation. It is unlikely to be Lottie. Let us suppose that on iOS these are Metal shaders. And what about Android? It is implemented there just as beautifully and smoothly. Is Telegram really switching to Rive 🤔
