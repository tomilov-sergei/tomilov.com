This concept is deeper than it seems. Let us put the cards on the table already. Objective reality does not exist. This is a fact. We live in a world of interpretations, in a world of hallucinations. The most powerful neural network (for now) inside the skull is occupied with a continuous process of explaining to itself what is happening around. It draws itself a map of the universe from everything that comes to hand. So at the moment when the skull wants to find out where the nearest metro is, does it care whether it is looking at a graphic interface that tries to pretend to be a military scheme (because that is more familiar), or whether the interface resembles frames from a favorite cartoon? It does not care.
